## Overview

**JetIntel** is a business aviation intelligence platform designed for mission planners and aviation professionals. It evaluates flight requirements with AI, compares aircraft performance metrics, and provides detailed fleet data to support informed jet selection decisions.

## Platform Capabilities

### **AI Mission Evaluation**
- Describe your travel requirements (range, passengers, budget)
- GPT-powered matching against the full fleet database
- Ranked recommendations with justification for each aircraft

### **Fleet Intelligence**
- Detailed specifications for the world's premier business jets
- Performance metrics: range, speed, passenger capacity, ceiling
- Price benchmarks and market positioning

### **Performance Analytics**
- Speed, range, and efficiency comparisons across aircraft classes
- Ultra Long Range vs. Super Midsize vs. Light jet trade-offs
- Visual performance comparison charts

### **Cost Analysis**
- Annual operating costs and hourly rates
- Fuel efficiency breakdowns by aircraft
- Mission cost estimation

### **Distance Calculator**
- Airport-to-airport nautical mile calculations
- Range overlay to filter aircraft by route viability
