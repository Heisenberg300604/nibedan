## Overview

**Athleto** is a hackathon-born platform designed to solve one of India's biggest talent pipeline problems: underprivileged athletes with world-class potential lack access to funding, visibility, and sponsors. Athleto bridges that gap.

## Key Features

### **AI Talent Scouting**
- Performance data analysis to surface athletes who would otherwise go unnoticed
- Standardized scoring across disciplines for fair comparison

### **Gamified Crowdfunding**
- Campaign creation for athletes with milestone-based funding unlocks
- Community engagement through progress tracking and social sharing

### **Sponsorship Matching**
- Intelligent algorithm connecting athletes to brands aligned with their sport and demographics
- Sponsor dashboards with campaign ROI analytics

### **Platform**
- Built with Next.js App Router and TypeScript for type safety and performance
- Supabase handles auth, database, and storage
- Deployed on Vercel with CI/CD
