## Overview

**Recap** is an AI-powered study companion that won **1st Place at NS Hacks (Namespace BPIT)**. It revolutionizes how students organize and interact with their study materials through intelligent automation.

## Key Features

### **Smart Note Organization**
- Automatic categorization by subject and topic
- AI-powered tagging for fast retrieval

### **OCR Integration**
- Convert handwritten notes to searchable digital text using Tesseract.js
- Supports photos of notebooks, textbooks, and whiteboards

### **Audio Transcription**
- Convert lecture recordings to searchable text via AssemblyAI
- Timestamp-indexed transcripts for navigation

### **Flashcard Generation**
- Automatic creation of study flashcards from notes
- Spaced repetition system for efficient revision

### **Gamified Learning**
- Study streaks and achievement badges
- Interactive AI-powered quizzes
- Collaborative study groups
